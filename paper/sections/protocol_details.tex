\appendix
\section{Frozen Domain Protocols}
\label{app:protocol}

For Crystal multi-response OMP, step $k$ retains margin $\Delta_k$, residual
$R_k$, and the largest aligned response displacement $\epsilon$ across seeds and
support offsets.  The preregistered conservative sufficient condition is
$\Delta_k>4\lVert R_k\rVert_F\epsilon+2\epsilon^2$; failure is not a necessary
instability result.  Across 25 cells, median/minimum decoder-projection $R^2$ are
$0.9967/0.0665$, motifs recur in at most one or two of five folds per seed,
0/300 certificates pass, and the affine-gauge audit fails.

Table~\ref{tab:domain-protocol} makes explicit the coordinate conventions that
are deliberately suppressed by a raw latent code.  Temperatures are sorted
before support selection.  The headline ZT and vapor-pressure least-squares
fits are unweighted float64 fits with
\texttt{numpy.linalg.lstsq(rcond=None)}.  The Crystal boundary fitter instead scales
support columns and solves the full-rank system by explicit float64 SVD.  The
confirmed ZT and vapor-pressure support matrices have at least five distinct
temperatures and numerical rank three for their three named columns.

Structure decisions use development query targets and are distinct from
external support-only inference.  ZT fixes the quadratic endpoint before its
five entity folds; it is not selected against its baselines.  Vapor compares
the coarse Clausius--Clapeyron basis with exactly one of
$\{\log(T/T_r),(T-T_r)/T_r,(1/T-1/T_r)^2\}$ in five DOI-grouped folds.  Lowest
median entity physical NRMSE wins; candidates within 1\% use that displayed
order.  This selects $\log(T/T_r)$.  The structure, reference-temperature rule,
support mask, metric, tie break, and analysis are then frozen before temporal
targets are opened; an external entity supplies only its support coefficients.

For Crystal routing, let the ordered spread-support slopes be
$s_i=(C_{p,i+1}-C_{p,i})/(T_{i+1}-T_i)$ and
\begin{equation}
  \rho_e =
  \frac{\max(0,s_{\mathrm{last}})}
  {\max\!\left\{\operatorname{median}_{i<\mathrm{last}}|s_i|,
  10^{-8}\max(1,\operatorname{median}|C_p|)/(T_{\max,e}-T_{\min,e})\right\}}.
\end{equation}
The boundary family is used when $\rho_e>\gamma$.  The reported OOF estimate
uses outer-training selections: fold 1 selects
$(\gamma,d,\delta)=(200,1,0.003)$, folds 2--4 select
$(100,2,0.0003)$, and fold 5 selects $(50,2,0.0003)$; every fold selects the
inverse-square-root atom.
The unopened final package separately freezes
$(\gamma,d,\delta)=(100,2,0.0003)$.

For all domains, entity NRMSE is
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{NRMSE}_e=
 \frac{\sqrt{|Q_e|^{-1}\sum_{i\in Q_e}(y_i-\hat y_i)^2}}
 {\sqrt{|Q_e|^{-1}\sum_{i\in Q_e}(y_i-\bar y_{Q_e})^2}},
\end{equation}
so the denominator is the population standard deviation (\texttt{ddof=0}).
The frozen Starry evaluation manifest, ThermoML single-use lock and source-hash
ledger, and Crystal single-use confirmation contract bind the corresponding
support/query tables, evaluators, and source contents by SHA-256.
