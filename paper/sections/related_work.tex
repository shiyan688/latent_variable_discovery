\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

% ROLE: support-conditioned function representation.
\paragraph{Few-shot function representations.}
Conditional Neural Processes encode a context set to predict targets of a new
function, while MetaFun performs iterative functional updates and function
contrastive learning optimizes transferable function-level representations
\citep{garnelo2018conditional,xu2020metafun,gondal2021function}.  MetaSDF and
Functa learn shared priors over implicit functions or compact modulations and
adapt instance-specific representations from observations; Functa also
demonstrates MAP completion from partial observations
\citep{sitzmann2020metasdf,dupont2022functa}.  These methods establish that
support-conditioned function prediction, low-dimensional task variables, and
test-time adaptation are not new.  Our question begins where their predictive
objective ends: if a task representation will enter a scientific equation,
which response-derived coordinate is invariant enough to name?  We therefore
evaluate CNP and functional baselines directly and interpret coefficients of a
declared physical response basis rather than an unconstrained context vector.

% ROLE: identifiability and canonicalization boundary.
\paragraph{Latent identifiability and invariant structure.}
Unsupervised disentanglement is generally non-identifiable without inductive
bias, although conditional nonlinear-ICA models identify latent sources up to
specified equivalence classes under explicit auxiliary-variable and generative assumptions
\citep{locatello2019challenging,khemakhem2020variational}.  Recent work shifts
attention from individual latent variables to invariant metric structures, and
canonicalization provides a broader framework for constructing invariant or
equivariant representations
\citep{syrota2025metric,ma2024canonicalization}.  Canonicalization can itself be
discontinuous for common group actions, motivating weighted frames and careful
scope statements \citep{dym2024equivariant}.  We neither claim generic
canonicalization nor recovery of true sources.  Our narrower object is the
equivalence class induced by agreement on declared physical probes; its fixed
linear projection is continuous in the probe response, whereas the independent
support optimizer retains explicit affine, rank, and conditioning assumptions.

% ROLE: scientific equation discovery boundary.
\paragraph{Learned coordinates and symbolic equations.}
SINDy autoencoders jointly learn nonlinear coordinates and sparse governing
dynamics, and neural-to-symbolic extraction recovers compact relations from
structured learned components
\citep{champion2019data,cranmer2020discovering}.  Parametric equation models
share symbolic structure while coefficients vary across parameterized
instances, and UPINNs
convert neural representations of unknown differential-equation terms into
symbolic expressions from sparse observations
\citep{zhang2022parametric,podina2023universal}.  LaSR uses an LLM-induced
library of abstract textual concepts to guide evolutionary symbolic regression
and improve benchmark performance \citep{grayeli2024lasr}.  Accordingly, neither
symbolic regression, fixed basis projection, nor shared equations with varying
coefficients is our novelty.  Our distinction is the audited interface around
them: exact prediction-preserving chart intervention, support-only coefficients
for unseen entities, response-space rank diagnostics, and single-use temporal
expression evaluation with interpolation baselines and entity tails.

% ROLE: positioning summary.
Together, these lines imply a deliberately bounded contribution.  We combine a
support-conditioned learned response, a basis-relative invariant explanatory
object, and sealed symbolic transfer; we do not assert that latent variables are
always unidentifiable, that our basis is a unique natural coordinate system, or
that a neural prior must improve prediction.  On the Crystal development
registry the frozen GIRD incremental gates do not pass, so we retain it as a
controlled diagnostic of when support and decoder information disagree rather
than presenting it as an improved predictor.
