## Verdict

The current manuscript is reasonably judged to have reached the top half of
the ICLR submission pool under its present, narrowed narrative. The reviewer
assigned a 55% probability, with a subjective 45--65% interval, of entering
that pool.

The assessment depends on the paper making the following bounded contribution:

- support-conditioned scientific curves admit auditable response coordinates
  relative to a frozen basis and probe specification;
- these coordinates support interpretable, externally evaluated expressions
  on ZT and vapor pressure and a development-only stage-wise expression on
  Crystal heat capacity;
- raw latent coordinates are not claimed to be unique physical variables;
- the failed learned Crystal bridge and GIRD result is retained as a
  falsifiable diagnostic rather than promoted as an improvement;
- strong local and functional baselines, uncertainty, tails, leakage checks,
  and development-versus-confirmation boundaries remain explicit.

The principal residual rejection risk is novelty and impact: a reviewer may
view the work as a careful protocol and representation audit rather than a
large new predictive algorithm. The still-unconsumed one-shot Crystal
confirmation could strengthen the package, but its absence is not an automatic
blocker for this bounded narrative.
