\subsection*{AI use statement}

Generative AI tools assisted with theoretical and conceptual development,
mathematical claims and proof drafts, hypothesis and experiment design, method
implementation, data cleaning, result interpretation, software and figure
generation, literature search and synthesis, reference formatting, and drafting
and editing the manuscript.  AI-assisted numerical claims were checked against
frozen raw artifacts and deterministic analysis code; generated code was
subjected to focused tests, leakage checks, hash-bound manifests, and independent
review where reported.  The authors take responsibility for the final content,
including all text, claims, code, and artifacts produced with AI assistance.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

Current repository artifacts include frozen cohort and decision manifests,
entity/DOI/time split definitions, support/query and target-perturbation audits
where reported, repository-local source SHA-256 values, and deterministic
analysis and figure generators.  Dataset snapshots and local source files are
identified by content hashes rather than mutable upstream links.  The
repository also contains the exact official-template build command.  An indexed,
allowlisted anonymous supplement was verified from its own root: every bundled
hash passes and it rebuilds a byte-identical manuscript.  It includes compact
claim evidence but not third-party source archives or training checkpoints;
their provenance and content hashes are supplied separately.
