\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% ROLE: limitations -- delimit the scientific and predictive claims before the takeaway.
Coordinates depend on probes, basis, weights, units, and support; they are not
unique or causal.  Misspecification and collinearity can hide or destabilize
coefficients despite high curve $R^2$.  All domains need target-valued support,
local methods can win, and Crystal's prior fails; conditioning, stability,
tails, negative predictions, and baselines therefore matter.

% ROLE: conclusion -- restate the solved representation problem and strongest evidence.
Within a frozen basis/probe specification, response coordinates are invariant
to prediction-equivalent charts; support calibration is affine-equivariant under
stated conditions.  This is an inference protocol, not universal canonicalization.
Despite Crystal's failed prior, support-only ZT and vapor equations transfer at
$R^2=0.9888$ and $0.9996$, with uncertainty and tails.
