# Fresh ICLR reviewer audit — 2026-08-29

## Verdict

A fresh, read-only Luna reviewer assigned the current package a subjective
`20%` probability of entering the ICLR top 50%, with a plausible range of
`15%--30%`. This is not an acceptance predictor; it is a prioritization signal.

The expression endpoint itself is not the problem. It is strictly entity-held
out/support-query, reaches development `R²=0.980668` and one-shot temporal
confirmation `R²=0.988810`, has exact zero query-target leakage, and need not
recover raw q or a unique law. The central rejection risk is the gap between
that support structure re-q result and a unified learned neural-q method.

## Strongest rejection argument

The only independently confirmed expression is a three-coefficient quadratic
estimated directly from support. Direct raw-q-to-coefficient transfer fails
(`R²=-1.907461`); the scale-aware neural functional bridge has strong pooled
accuracy (`R²=0.942488`) and stability but still fails its full tail gate on
9/80 entities. The expression does not show significant superiority over kNN
on the 30-material confirmation (`p=0.465`). A reviewer can therefore describe
the paper as standard support polynomial fitting plus an informative latent
failure analysis, rather than a complete new learning algorithm.

## Defensible response

- The scientific endpoint was explicitly narrower than predictive superiority:
  a compact, stage-wise suggestive physical expression under strict unseen-entity
  support-query evaluation. It is passed and temporally/materially confirmed.
- Raw-q failure is part of the scientific finding, not hidden. Decoder-functional
  projection changes the equation R² from `-1.907461` to `0.944683/0.942488`
  with minimum decoder fidelity above `0.984`.
- The scale-aware one-factor repair is diagnostic evidence rather than a
  post-hoc success relabel: median entity R² improves `0.846748 -> 0.940261`,
  tail failures `19 -> 9`, and functional/raw distance stability becomes
  `0.835/0.748`.
- kNN, FPCA, negative MATR outcomes, individual failures, and non-significant
  paired comparisons remain visible. The paper must claim compact transferable
  state and interpretability, not universal prediction SOTA.

## Corrected highest-value next action

The fresh reviewer proposed applying the scale-aware neural bridge to the 30
temporal-confirmation materials. That action is **not authorized**: the cohort
has a one-shot consumption receipt, its targets were already opened for the
frozen structure-re-q evaluator, and both bridge plans explicitly forbid reuse.
Reopening it would weaken rather than strengthen the evidence.

The highest-value valid choice is therefore:

1. if a genuinely new, target-blind external entity cohort becomes available,
   freeze the trained scale-aware decoder, q-calibration procedure, quadratic
   projection, thresholds, and hashes before accessing its query targets, then
   run one bridge confirmation; or
2. without new data, finish the paper around the honest combined contribution:
   raw-q gauge diagnosis, decoder-functional canonicalization in development,
   and independently confirmed support structure re-q, with the 9/80 neural
   tail as a stated limitation.

No further threshold tuning or uniform target compression on the same 80
development queries is justified.

## Recommended paper spine

> Predictive latent coordinates for multi-entity scientific response functions
> are gauge-ambiguous and can be unsuitable for symbolic reading. Projecting the
> decoder response into a fixed named basis creates equation coordinates that
> are readable and support-recalibratable. On Starry ZT, the resulting compact
> response expression transfers to temporally new, publication- and
> composition-disjoint materials; MATR and neural tail results expose the
> boundary between pooled interpretability and universal entity robustness.

## Claims forbidden in title/abstract

- recovery of a true, causal, unique, or original raw q;
- a fully passing or universally tail-safe neural-to-canonical bridge;
- general superiority over kNN, FPCA, or support-aware baselines;
- statistically significant Starry prediction superiority;
- completed MATR Batch3 confirmation;
- discovery of a true microscopic physical law;
- success on every material/entity;
- a claim that the temporal expression was automatically discovered from raw q.
